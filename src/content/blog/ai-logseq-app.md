I use Logseq on my laptop for note taking, and sync with my phone. However, the Logseq phone app is slow to load and somewhat annoying to use. 
So I had Claude make a simpler app with only the features I need to view my most frequented pages.

I gave it some short instructions, it immediately gave back an Android Studio project zip, then we made some modifications with Claude
doing the debugging and me styling the app to my preferences. (I asked it to list the changes it made per file instead of rezipping everything,
since our versions diverged. There's probably some better way to do this in Cowork mode.) The whole thing took less than an hour.

It's been a few days, and the app is very nice to use; I recommend using AI to make phone apps tailored to your specific needs (especially 
simple ones like note taking apps).
